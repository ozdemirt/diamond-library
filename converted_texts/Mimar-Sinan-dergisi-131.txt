Finance. The summon is sent to Bro. Rıza Tevfik, vvho then was the Grand Orator
and several years later will become the Grand Master, vvith a personal note. This
note is a request to attend the meeting and give some information about the
Brethren which he met in his recent journey to Europe.

Mustafa Asım. Bro. Mustafa Asım was initiated in WL Macedonia Risorta and now
he is raised to the Master's degree in WL Light of East [Ziya-i Şark]. This certificate is

can be made. They are some kind of unwritten laws.
