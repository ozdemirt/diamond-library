HISTORY OF THE MILITARY MASONRY
As all Masons are aware, one of the basic principles of Freemasonry is the loyalty to
your country and state and obedience to laws. Courage is emphasised amongst the
Four Basic Virtues of Masonry. The social duty of a Mason can be depicted as the
settlement of a humanity living in peace supported by his affection to his country.
However, in order to sustain peace, sometimes war may be essential. As will be se­
en below, throughout history, Masons, especially Masons in the armies, have pla­
yed an important role and many have lost their lives in defending their country.
Many have fought and died in battles for a sustainable peace on Earth. Grand Master
Faik Suleyman Pasha outcomes as a typical example who lost list life in August 7,
1916 during a battle in the Caucasean Front while defending his country against the
Russians.
